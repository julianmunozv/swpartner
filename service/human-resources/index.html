Human Resources

We can help reduce your fixed costs, minimize risks by handling your HR and payroll needs. We can assist you with:

  • HR Administration: vacation records and termination.
  • Payroll service: accurate, on time and discrete, according to the Chilean labor laws. It includes calculation and payment of personnel taxes, Chilean Social security system and mandatory health insurance for all employees.
  • Coordinating electronic payment with the bank.
  • Affiliation to the Chilean Compensation Fund (“Sistema de Caja de Compensación”) if required, as a benefit for employees.
